Memy Test Flows

Memy prototype: 8 flows to test

About 15 minutes on a phone. Each flow ends with the one question I'd like your answer to.

Open the prototype

Tap the tiny "start over" label at the very top at any time to reset. Any 6 digits work as the code. Nothing is real: no messages are sent, nothing is paid.

01 First minute 3 min

Does the "ask their twin" moment land before you've seen anything else?

  1. Start over. Get started, phone, code, your name.
  2. Name your twin. Try "super" and the plain one.
  3. Allow WhatsApp and Gmail. Wait for it to read.
  4. On "link your twin", accept Jonas, request Mia.
  5. On "ask Noah's twin", tap what's their address? then free this weekend?
Question: after the address refusal, did you understand that their rules said no, not Memy? And which twin name felt right once you saw it used in a sentence?

02 Home as the swarm 2 min

Grok-style home: agents and twins as faces with states.

  1. Land on home. Look at the lightbulb on Memy, the badge on your twin, the dimmed people.
  2. Tap Noah's face. Ask anything. Go back.
  3. Tap hire, hire the booker. Back home: it's now in the top row.
Question: is it clear which faces are your agents and which are other people's twins? Should they be on the same screen at all?

03 Memy speaks up 2 min

Your "pro-active private messages with evidence nodes".

  1. Tap the octopus on home.
  2. Read the clash: you told Noah you're free, calendar says dinner with Mia. Check the two evidence cards.
  3. Tap tell noah's twin i'm busy. See what comes back.
  4. On the memory, tap send the card. Hearts are only for you.
  5. Type why do you talk to me? and send.
Question: is this the right tone for Memy, and is "evidence first, then one-tap action" the right shape for every proactive message?

04 Someone asks about you 2 min

The inbox: what your twin did on its own, what it held, what it blocked.

  1. Burger menu, asked about you.
  2. Open the food one (needs you). Tap send it + ask back "what time?". Follow it into the thread.
  3. Open the 3am doctor one (answered on its own). Read why it went out.
  4. Open the stranger's address one (blocked).
Question: three buckets, needs you / on its own / blocked. Enough? Too many?

05 Never, then grants 3 min

Your ER doctor case. Defaults stay "never", exceptions are grants.

  1. Burger, you · rules, then held back · grants.
  2. Read the two seeded grants. Tap add: medication list, to any verified doctor, always. Add it.
  3. Tap a grant and revoke it.
  4. Back on home, tap Noah, ask any allergies?. Note the reason line under the answer.
Question: does "never by default, exceptions by grant: what, to whom, how long" cover the cases you had in mind? What's missing?

06 Linking twins 2 min

Twins don't know each other at the start. Both sides say yes.

  1. Tap your twin on home. Type how did my Mia's interview go?
  2. Tap link twins with mia. Wait about six seconds.
  3. Watch the chat pick the question up on its own once she accepts.
  4. Open people, tap Mia, tap pause · unlink, pause.
Question: is "link" the right word? Should a link request carry a note, or stay silent like this?

07 Someone not on Memy 1 min

The bridge: twin drafts, you send.

  1. Tap your twin. Type is dad free friday?
  2. Tap draft it for dad. Then add a memory card to it.
  3. Tap preview what they see.
Question: is the memory card the right invite, or should the plain draft be enough?

08 The crew 2 min

Your "more agents" ask, kept under one Memy.

  1. Tap your twin. Type book a table friday. Hire the booker when offered.
  2. Ask again. Tap yes, book it. Popcorn.
  3. Burger, crew. Read Memy's line at the top.
Question: is "your twin hires a crew, Memy referees all of them" the right frame for more agents? Would you want to see agents from other companies in that list?
